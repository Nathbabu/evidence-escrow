How a case moves

account_tree
01

File the deal

Payer names the payee and writes the terms in plain language, then deposits the funds. Nothing leaves escrow until this is settled.

02

Agree, or don't

If both sides agree it's done, one click releases the full amount. No AI, no waiting, no dispute needed.

03

If not, evidence decides

Each side submits their account, plus an optional link. GenLayer validators independently read it and rule on a refund split, not a guess.
