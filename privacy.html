Privacy

Privacy policy

EvidenceEscrow is a static site that runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to GenLayer's public testnet. There is no backend server, no account system, and no company database behind it.

visibility_off

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. There are no forms, no sign-ups, no email addresses, and no analytics or tracking scripts anywhere on this site.

cookie

Cookies

This site does not use cookies.

database

Local storage

Your “My deals” list is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere and is not accessible to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

public

On-chain data

Deal terms, addresses, submitted evidence, and rulings are read directly from GenLayer's public testnet. This is public by nature of how a blockchain works, not something this site chooses to publish, and it's equally accessible to anyone querying the same contract address directly.

wallet

Wallet connections

Connecting a wallet (such as MetaMask) lets this site request transactions for you to review and approve. Your private key and seed phrase remain in your wallet extension at all times and are never transmitted to or seen by this site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the project's repository.
